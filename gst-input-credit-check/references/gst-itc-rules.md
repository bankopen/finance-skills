# GST input credit rules this skill relies on

Status: **DRAFT, needs chartered accountant review before publication.** Last checked against secondary sources: 30 Oct 2026. The primary text (CGST Act, CGST Rules, CBIC notifications) governs.

| Rule | Statement used by the skill | Where to verify |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice must appear in GSTR-2B | Input credit is claimed only to the extent the invoice appears in your auto-drafted GSTR-2B | Section 16(2)(aa) CGST Act; Rule 36(4) |
| 180-day payment | If the supplier is not paid within 180 days of the invoice date, the credit is reversed (with interest) and may be re-claimed after payment | Second proviso to section 16(2); Rule 37 |
| Claim time limit | Credit cannot be claimed after the earlier of 30 November following the financial year of the invoice or the date of the annual return | Section 16(4) |
| Re-claim after reversal | Credit reversed under Rule 37 can be re-availed after payment | Rule 37 |
| Blocked credits | Certain goods and services are blocked | Section 17(5) |
| Supplier non-filing reversal | Open: sources conflict on the exact trigger and date | Check Rule 37A text before relying on it |

Do not add a rule here without its section reference and a date.
