# Statutory dates used by close-calendar

Status: **DRAFT, needs chartered accountant review before publication.** Last compiled from general knowledge and secondary sources: 30 Oct 2026. Due dates are changed by notification, extensions and holidays. Never rely on this file alone.

| Item | Usual date (verify) | Source to check |
|---|---|---|
| GSTR-1 (monthly filers) | 11th of the next month | CBIC notifications, GST portal calendar |

| TDS deposit | 7th of the next month (30 April for March) | Income-tax rules; CBDT |
| TDS returns | Quarterly, by 31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May | CBDT |
| Provident fund and ESIC | 15th of the next month | EPFO and ESIC portals |
| Advance tax | 15 June (15%), 15 September (45%), 15 December (75%), 15 March (100%), cumulative | Income-tax Act; see also the advance tax calculator |
